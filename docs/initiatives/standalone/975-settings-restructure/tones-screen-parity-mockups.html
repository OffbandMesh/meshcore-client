Owner decisions, 2026-10-25

1. Screen and Battery under Node Settings

They are this radio's own hardware settings, beside Button actions, and they appear only on radios that have them. The rejected alternative was a new top-level "Device display" category, which would be empty on most radios.

2. Play is a separate button

The badge auditions as you move because moving and choosing are separate keys. On a phone, a tap is the choice, so play is its own button and the name chooses. A preview never changes the setting.

3. No way back to "radio default"

Firmware refuses 0xFF on an event because the badge's picker has no Default row for events, and Screen off accepts only its five steps. The app matches that. A way back would be a firmware change.

4. "Menus and lists" and "Messages"

The badge says "Nav text" and "Message text", which fit 21 columns. The app has room to say what each governs.

Why these choices

The radio is the record, everywhere

Every reply on this list re-reads after the write: EVTONE and CHTUNE carry stored and effective, SCREEN carries the whole set. The app draws only from replies, stores none of it, and so has nothing per radio to isolate (#853) or to drift.

The app offers exactly what the badge offers

Firmware refuses anything its own screens would not offer. Building each list from the same rule (named built-ins plus filled slots, five Screen off steps, three sizes, the zone table) means the app never offers a choice the radio will reject.
