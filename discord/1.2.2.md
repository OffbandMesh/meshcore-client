🧵 **Offband Meshcore 1.2.2** is up, a follow-up to 1.2.1.

1.2.1 pinned your database to one place, but if you'd run several different builds on **Windows or Linux**, some history could still be sitting in other database files. 1.2.2 finds those on launch and merges them back into your main database, so history that still looked missing comes home.

Your old databases are never touched, and if anything goes wrong it just retries next launch. Android and the web app aren't affected.

🌐 **Web app:** https://offband.app
