🧪 **Testing build: Offband Meshcore 1.5.0-beta.1** is on the closed test track.

This is the first beta of the 1.5.0 line. It's a testing build, so treat it as not-yet-stable, and your bug reports are exactly what make the production release solid.

**What's new to poke at:**

🔓 **Experimental section.** A hidden **7-tap on the Build row** in settings unlocks an Experimental area. Turning the section back off clears every experimental toggle.

🛰️ **OKIMesh CoreScope (Experimental).** See how many **OKIMesh CoreScope** observers heard each packet, with an observer/observations badge and tap or long-press to refresh. This is tied specifically to OKIMesh's CoreScope, not a generic backend. **Heads-up:** it needs a **forthcoming Offband firmware** (not available for download yet) and stays inert until you're on it. It's here so it's ready to test the moment that firmware ships.

ℹ️ **About screen.** About now has its own Settings category with links to the website, Play listing, and donate page.

🛠️ **Fixes:** steadier repeater CLI (late replies, timeout budgets), broker import no longer touches the enabled state or leaks a key line, and channel "Send Again" gating.

Not on the test track yet and want in? Ask here. Thanks for testing. 📡
