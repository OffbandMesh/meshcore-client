🚀 **Offband Meshcore 1.7.0 is out.** A foundations release: faster storage, every radio keeps its own data, and a contact list that matches your radio. Over 100 changes since 1.6.0.

⚠️ **Read this before updating: it's a one-way upgrade.** On first launch, 1.7.0 converts your message history to a new database format. After that, **older versions of the app can't open it**, so there's no going back to 1.6.0. Your messages carry over. The old copy is kept 30 days and then cleared automatically, so the database is bigger for a while. Want the space back now? **App Settings > Storage > Clear Stale History Now** (it asks first and never touches your messages).

💾 **Storage that keeps up.** One row per message instead of one giant blob per conversation, so a new message costs the same at 50,000 messages of history as at 50, and the heavy lifting is off the UI thread.

📻 **Every radio keeps its own data.** Discovered nodes, blocked contacts, channel notification modes, path history, and delivery records all belong to the radio they came from. A brand-new radio no longer shows up pre-blocked, and switching radios no longer flashes the last radio's history.

👥 **Contacts that match your radio.** No more phantom contacts piling up from adverts your radio rejected; a contact your radio actually stores still shows up on its own. And **custom names**: rename any contact, keep seeing what the node calls itself underneath, and your label never goes over the air or into exports.

🗺️ **Region scoping.** Discover a repeater's regions and scope a channel to one, so its messages flood only there. Needs MeshCore companion 1.16+ or current Offband firmware.

⚙️ **Settings, reorganized.** Location gets its own category (and is the one home for GPS), Buzzer and tones moves to Message Settings, and import/export plus GPX live under Actions. Badge tones, custom tunes, screen settings, and GPS power save are built in and will appear once the matching Offband firmware ships.

🖥️ **CLI for stock 1.18 companions.** A Device CLI console for your radio, remote CLI to another companion from the chat menu, and a per-contact Remote CLI permission toggle. CLI stays out of your chat.

📶 **Steadier Bluetooth.** Auto-reconnect now gives up after 5 tries or 3 minutes, **Stop** ends it right away, and a connect you start always wins. Plus a fix for a race that could leave the composer unusable, and a Windows crash on close.

🔧 **Fixes.** Messages with 2- or 3-byte path hashes show the right hop count (no more 13 hops reading as 39).
