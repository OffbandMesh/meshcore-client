🚀 **Offband Meshcore 1.6.0 is out.** Our biggest update yet, and the stable successor to 1.4.0 on Google Play. Over 100 changes since the beta. The big ones:

👥 **Contacts, reworked.**
- Tap a contact shared on a channel to **Add Contact**, or share your own contact into a channel.
- Add someone by **public key** (type/paste, or key-only before an advert), or by **QR** (show and scan).
- Full **cross-client with stock MeshCore**: contact shares move both ways.
- Each contact shows **how confirmed its identity is**, and you can add/view/block a channel sender right from their avatar.

🧳 **Bring your setup with you.** Stock-compatible **config export/import** and **identity export/import**, so moving between radios (or in and out of stock) just works.

📻 **Radio presets.** A region-grouped **preset picker** labeled by source (credit to Liam Cottle / MeshCore for the upstream presets). Apply a preset's path hash to a companion, observer, repeater, or room server, with region matching that won't mislabel a USA radio as Canada.

🔁 **Sync that heals itself.** Offband now detects a short contact sync and recovers it automatically, tells you the recovered count and which contacts are genuinely gone, and (importantly) **never overwrites your saved contacts** on a short or unreadable sync.

📶 **Bluetooth reliability, big pass.** The composer no longer locks up from an unreported BLE MTU: negotiated MTU is now reported on **Windows, Linux, and web**, and if a link truly can't carry a message it tells you why instead of going dead. Plus bounded GATT-133 retry with readable errors, disconnects that retry-and-warn instead of vanishing, and closed GATT handle leaks.

✉️ **Messaging fixes.** Delivered DMs are no longer marked failed (send correlation now uses the radio's own ACK hash), and late repeater replies go to the log instead of your screen.

➕ Plus an **About screen**, **top-of-screen notifications**, USB serial that doesn't drop bytes, and (experimental) **OKIMesh CoreScope** per-packet observer counts.
