🧪 **Offband MeshCore 1.8.1 is out for beta testers:** on Android through the Google Play test track, and on iPhone through TestFlight. Contacts you can count on, direct messages that find their way back, and a way to send us your logs when something goes wrong.

⚠️ **Heads up before updating.** "Direct" was removed from a contact's path options, because Auto already reaches a radio in range directly and Direct blocked the flood fallback. A contact set to Direct goes back to Auto, and a hand-picked path is kept. Direct messages now try 3 times instead of 5, waiting at least 12.5 seconds each time. And on first start, 1.8.1 moves aside channel messages an older version copied into the wrong radio after a Bluetooth switch (moved, not deleted).

📨 **Fewer "failed" direct messages that actually arrived.** When a contact's stored route goes bad, the last attempt floods, and the reply teaches both radios a working route again. Before, that fallback was behind a setting that was off by default, so a message could arrive and still show as failed.

👥 **Contacts.** A contact is kept only when your radio confirms the add. A full radio asks you to remove someone, prune stale contacts, or cancel, and nothing changes until you choose. **Prune stale contacts** clears out ones you haven't heard from in 15, 30 or 90 days, never favorites, and keeps their messages.

🩺 **Send logs to Offband.** From the log screens or Settings > Debug, send the app's logs straight to us, encrypted so only Offband can read them, with passwords and PINs removed. Add a note and, if you'd like a reply, your name and email. After a crash or freeze, the next launch offers to send them. You get a reference code to mention when you report the problem.

📡 **Also:** switching Bluetooth radios no longer mixes up their messages, and Settings can delete a radio's message history. Node locations follow the newest advert. Sending the same text twice shows two messages. On Mac, both apps are named Offband MeshCore and the Mac app can save files again. New text is English-only for now in other languages.

🐛 **Known issues** (full list in the release notes): a channel's phone notifications set to Off can still notify, contacts can still disappear with overwrite off (we now log every removal to find out why), and a factory reset only clears the app data on the phone that sent it.
