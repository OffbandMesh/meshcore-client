Offband MeshCore 1.8.1

New: send logs to Offband, encrypted, from Settings or after a crash.
Direct messages flood when their route fails and wait longer for delivery. The Direct path option was removed: Auto already reaches nearby radios.
Contacts are kept only when your radio confirms them, a full radio asks before removing one, and you can prune stale contacts.
Switching radios no longer mixes up their messages.
Locations follow the newest advert.
Sending a text twice shows two messages.
