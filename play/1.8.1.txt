Offband MeshCore 1.8.1

New: send the app's logs to Offband, encrypted, from Settings or after a crash.
Direct messages flood when their route fails and wait longer for delivery. The Direct path option is gone.
A contact is kept only when your radio confirms it, a full radio asks before one is removed, and you can prune stale contacts.
Switching Bluetooth radios no longer mixes up their messages.
Locations follow the newest advert.
Sending the same text twice shows two messages.
