## Offband MeshCore 1.8.1

A contacts, delivery and diagnostics release. The app keeps a contact only when your radio
confirms it, asks before a full radio drops anyone, and no longer files one radio's messages
under another after a Bluetooth switch. Direct messages now fall back to a flood when their
route has gone bad, and wait long enough for the delivery confirmation to arrive. And you can
now send the app's logs to Offband, encrypted, when something goes wrong.

### Please read before updating

- **Copied messages are moved aside once.** Earlier versions could save one radio's channel
  messages into another radio's channel after a Bluetooth switch. On first start, 1.8.1 moves
  those copies to a separate store. It doesn't delete them, and a copy it can't match to an
  original stays where it is. To clear what's left for a radio, use Settings > Actions >
  **Delete message history** (#1163).
- **"Direct" is gone from a contact's path options.** A contact set to Direct goes back to
  Auto, which already settles on the direct route for a radio in range. A hand-picked path is
  kept (#1223).
- **Direct messages try 3 times, not 5, and wait longer each time.** Each attempt waits at
  least 12.5 seconds for the delivery confirmation, so a message gives up after about the same
  time as before while sending less on a shared mesh. If you never changed Max Message
  Retries, it moves from 5 to 3. The slider still goes from 2 to 10 (#316).
- **A full radio asks first.** When your radio's contact list is full, adding a contact asks
  you to remove a suggested contact, prune stale ones, or cancel. Nothing is sent to the radio
  until you choose (#1028).
- **The Remote CLI switch shows only on radios that support it.** No Offband firmware release
  does yet. Stock MeshCore 1.18 or newer does (#1155).
- **New text is in English only.** The 67 new messages and labels in this release are English
  in every language for now.

### Send logs to Offband

- **Send to Offband** sits next to Share on both log screens and in Settings > Debug. It sends
  the app's logs, including your messages and the radio traffic, with passwords and PINs
  removed. They are encrypted on your device so only Offband can read them, and deleted 30
  days after we review them. The screen says this before you send (#1190, #1193).
- You can add a note on what happened, and your name or screen name and email. All three are
  optional. Without contact details we can't get back to you with an update.
- A sent package gets a reference code, with a Copy button. Mention it when you report the
  problem so we can find your logs. If a send fails, the reason stays on screen and **Retry**
  sends the same package again.
- **After a crash, an "app not responding" or an unexpected close**, the next launch offers to
  send the logs: Send, Not now, or Don't ask again. When the system closes the app in the
  background to free memory, it doesn't ask. **Send crash logs automatically** is off unless
  you turn it on in Settings > Debug (#1194).

### Direct messages

- **A failed route now falls back to a flood.** The radio sends a direct message along the
  route it has stored for that contact. When that route has gone bad, the last attempt now
  floods instead, and the reply that comes back teaches both radios a working route again.
  Before, that fallback sat behind **Auto route rotation**, which was off by default, so
  messages could arrive and still show as failed (#1223, #1198).
- Auto route rotation and **Clear path on max retry** are removed, because the fallback now
  always runs. The route weight and Max Message Retries sliders they hid are shown, since
  they applied to every send anyway (#1223).
- A hand-picked path is still used for every attempt but the last. If you change it while a
  message is sending, the new path goes to the radio (#1223).
- Attempts are counted from 1, so the last one no longer reads as if another is still to come
  (#1223).

### Contacts

- An add is kept only when the radio says OK. If the radio refuses it or doesn't answer within
  5 seconds, the app says which, and the message stays until you dismiss it. A pasted contact
  that doesn't parse says "Invalid contact data" (#1028).
- A direct message the radio refuses fails at once, with the reason on the message, instead
  of after the delivery timeout (#1028).
- **Prune stale contacts**, in the Contacts menu and in the full-radio prompt, lists contacts
  that aren't favorites and haven't been heard for 15, 30 or 90 days. It removes the ticked
  ones one at a time, each confirmed by the radio, and keeps their direct messages (#1182).
- When the radio overwrites a contact to make room, the app moves it to Discovery, keeps its
  messages, and names it in a banner (#1028).

### Messages

- Switching between Bluetooth radios no longer files the previous radio's channel messages
  under the new one (#1163).
- Settings > Actions > **Delete message history** removes the connected radio's channel and
  direct message history. Clear Chat now deletes the stored history too (#1163).
- A channel write the radio didn't answer waits for a re-read before saying "not confirmed",
  and the error offers **Resync**. The Channels screen has a Resync action (#1163).
- Sending the same channel text twice in a row shows two messages, not one with a repeat count
  (#1183).
- On Windows over Bluetooth, a radio's quick reply could be missed and reported as no reply.
  Fixed (#1028).

### Map and paths

- A node's location is what its newest advert says, including no location. 0,0 counts as
  unknown and isn't plotted. With no newer advert, the last known location stays, and Packet
  Path shows its age (#1166).

### CLI console

- Settings shows the Device CLI, and contacts get the CLI console and the Remote CLI switch,
  only on radios that say they support them. Stock MeshCore 1.18 or newer gets both. No
  Offband firmware release supports them yet, so on Offband radios they stay hidden. A
  command sent to a radio without CLI support is refused with an error (#1155).

### Apple

- The iPhone app and the Mac app are both named Offband MeshCore. On a Mac the iPhone app
  showed as "Runner" (#1140).
- Running the iPhone app on a Mac shows a one-time notice that it's the iPhone version
  (#1140).
- Mac app: the Save dialog works, so logs and exports can be saved again (#1143).

### For troubleshooting

- Every Bluetooth drop the system reports is logged with its reason where the platform gives
  one (iPhone, Mac, Android), the time since the radio last sent anything, and whether the app
  was in the background. App lifecycle changes are logged too (#1186).
- Every contact removal is logged with the contact's name, whether it was a favorite, and
  what removed it. This is the first step on contacts that disappear (#1167, #1170).

### Behind the scenes

- The app manual's first pages, with screenshots, and a Set Region Scope guide, published to
  offband.org (#1145, #1148, #1232, #1236).
- Releases are sent to Google Play from CI, only for a bundle built by the signed release run
  (#1053).
- CI requires a code change to come with a test that fails on the old code (#1173).

### Known issues

- **Phone notifications set to Off for a channel can still notify** (#1121).
- **Contacts, including favorites, can disappear** with overwrite turned off. The new removal
  log is there to find out why (#1167).
- **Factory reset from one phone leaves that radio's old data on your other phones** and PCs
  (#1123).
- **On a radio with a screen but no switchable LED** (the SenseCAP T1000-E, the QCC badge, the
  Heltec T096) running Offband firmware 1.7.0, the Status LED switch still appears and the
  radio turns the change down (#1132).
- **A Display or LED change can be undone** by the radio's older reading arriving just after
  it (#1124).
- **On a SenseCAP T1000-E, Position Reporting Interval says "Location updated"** even though
  the radio refused the value (#1134).
- **On Windows, Linux and the web, a Bluetooth drop is logged without a reason**, because
  those platforms don't report one (#1187).
