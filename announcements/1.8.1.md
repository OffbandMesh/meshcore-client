**Title:** Offband MeshCore 1.8.1

---

Offband MeshCore 1.8.1 is out. It's a contacts, delivery and diagnostics release: a contact is
kept only when your radio confirms it, direct messages fall back to a flood when their route
has gone bad, and you can send the app's logs to Offband, encrypted, when something goes
wrong.

Full release notes, downloads and known issues:
https://github.com/OffbandMesh/meshcore-client/releases/tag/v1.8.1

### Before you update

- **Copied messages are moved aside once.** On first start, 1.8.1 moves channel messages that
  an older version copied into the wrong radio after a Bluetooth switch to a separate store.
  Nothing is deleted. Settings > Actions > **Delete message history** clears a radio's
  history.
- **"Direct" was removed from a contact's path options,** because Auto already reaches a
  radio in range directly, and Direct blocked the flood fallback. A contact set to Direct goes
  back to Auto. A hand-picked path is kept.
- **Direct messages try 3 times, not 5,** and each attempt waits at least 12.5 seconds for
  the delivery confirmation.
- **The Remote CLI switch shows only on radios that support it.** No Offband firmware release
  does yet; stock MeshCore 1.18 or newer does.
- **New text is English-only for now** in the other 17 languages.

### What's new

- **Send logs to Offband:** from the log screens or Settings > Debug. The logs include your
  messages and the radio traffic, with passwords and PINs removed, encrypted so only Offband
  can read them, and deleted 30 days after review. A note, your name and your email are
  optional. After a crash, an "app not responding" or an unexpected close, the next launch
  offers to send them. Sending automatically is off unless you turn it on.
- **Direct messages:** the last attempt floods when the stored route fails, which also lets
  both radios relearn a working route. Auto route rotation and Clear path on max retry are
  removed because the fallback always runs now.
- **Contacts:** an add is kept only when the radio confirms it. A full radio asks before
  anyone is removed. Prune stale contacts removes ones not heard in 15, 30 or 90 days, never
  favorites, and keeps their messages. A direct message the radio refuses fails at once with
  the reason.
- **Messages:** switching Bluetooth radios no longer files one radio's messages under
  another. A channel write the radio didn't answer offers Resync. Sending the same channel
  text twice shows two messages.
- **Map:** a node's location follows its newest advert, and Packet Path shows how old it is.
- **Apple:** both apps are named Offband MeshCore, the iPhone app on a Mac says once that it's
  the iPhone version, and the Mac app can save files again.
- **Troubleshooting:** Bluetooth drops are logged with the reason the system gives, and every
  contact removal is logged by name.

### Known issues

- A channel's phone notifications set to Off can still notify (#1121).
- Contacts, including favorites, can disappear with overwrite turned off (#1167).
- A factory reset deletes the app data only on the phone that sent it (#1123).
- On a T1000-E, QCC badge or Heltec T096 running firmware 1.7.0, the Status LED switch
  appears but the radio refuses it (#1132).
- A Display or LED change can be undone by an older reading arriving just after it (#1124).
- On a T1000-E, Position Reporting Interval says it saved when the radio refused it (#1134).
- On Windows, Linux and the web, a Bluetooth drop is logged without a reason (#1187).
